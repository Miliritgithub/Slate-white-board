Built for teams who think in pictures: Product Design Engineering Education Remote teams

Everything happens at once, for everyone

No refreshing, no "final_v3" files. What you draw, everyone sees — as you draw it.

Live cursors & presence

See exactly where your teammates are on the board, what they're pointing at, and what they're about to change.

An infinite canvas

Zoom out for the big picture, zoom into the detail. There's always more room — the board grows with the idea.

Ink, shapes & sticky notes

Freehand pen, clean shapes, and notes you can drag around — the tools your hands already know how to use.

Instant replay

Scrub back through a session and watch the idea take shape, stroke by stroke — great for catching up latecomers.

Comments without the interruption

Drop a note next to a shape instead of a Slack message. It stays with the idea, not buried in a channel.

One link to share

Send the link and anyone can join and start drawing — no account, no download, no waiting on an invite.

From blank board to shared plan in four steps

1

Open a board

Start from a blank canvas or a template for retros, sprints, or lesson plans.

2

Share the link

Anyone with it can join and draw instantly — no sign-up required for guests.

3

Sketch together

Everyone's cursor, ink, and edits sync live — no locking, no waiting your turn.

4

Wrap up & export

Save the board as a PDF or image, or just leave it running for next time.
